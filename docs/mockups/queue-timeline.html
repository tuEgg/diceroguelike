Enemy moves in the queue: options

The same fight in each: you have built 3 slots (Attack 6, Block 5, Attack 4, plus the place bonus), and three gulls act at different points. Gull A strikes after your slot 1, Gull B buffs after slot 2, Gull C attacks after slot 3 (which you haven't built yet, so it acts at the end). The queue resolves left to right, yours and theirs mixed. The numbers in small circles are the order things happen.
