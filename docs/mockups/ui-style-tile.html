Everything you read is ink on paper. Everything you play with sits on the dark table. Gold ink only ever means insight. The one commit action sits bottom right, numbers are louder than words, every colour also has a shape, and anything you don't need at a glance waits for a hover.

What changes, screen by screen

  1. Kit first: palette, type and components in scUI; old helpers call into it, so every screen improves as it moves over.
  2. Combat: as above.
  3. Map: already closest. Tidy the top bar, the draft heading, the strait labels; routes and stops in the same ink.
  4. One card everywhere: bag, shop shelf, rewards, the quartermaster and the bounty board on a single card layout.
  5. Rooms: events as a logbook entry with inked choices; the shop as a counter; the workbench as a bench; treasure as a chest on paper.
  6. Menus: main menu, pause, settings and the voyage summary on paper.

Each step lands as its own commit with before and after screenshots for you, and the branch stays mergeable throughout. Out of scope: new art for dice, enemies and backgrounds (F50, F42), and sound.
