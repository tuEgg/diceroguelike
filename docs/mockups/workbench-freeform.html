How each tool reads

  • Drill: drop a core on the drill (on the rack or the table) and its gem sits in the head. Drag the drill onto a die: it plunges, spins, throws sparks in the core's colour, and the gem sinks into the die.
  • Hammer: drag it over the gap between two dice of the same type. A mark shows where it will land; let go and it swings once. Both dice jump in and one bigger die is left right under the head.
  • Cutters: drag onto a die and let go. Snip: the die loses two faces and a coin of its type hops out beside it. A d4 cut becomes a coin itself.
  • Tools fly back to the rack after use. Dice dropped on the bag go back in.
