Wet ink edge (shader mockup)

Anything you can spend ink on in the ink view gets a wet edge: ink bleeding out from it, darker where it pools at the rim, with a moving shine and a slow creep. Here it's on the Reroll/Redraw buttons, the timeline marker's arrows, Reveal/Rewrite and the inkwell. In the game it would be one extra step in the ink view's shader pass, drawn from a mask of those shapes.
