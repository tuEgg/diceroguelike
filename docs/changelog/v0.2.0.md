# Tides of Fortune v0.2.0

The biggest update yet: dice now roll as they're dealt, insight becomes ink, the whole game has a new logbook look, and the map has been redesigned. Start a new voyage to see it all.

## Combat: rolled dice
- Dice roll as they're dealt, so you see every number before you play it.
- Slots show their total as you fill them. Click a die in a slot to take it back.
- You start with one slot. Build more on the new-slot tile from dice that add up to its number. Each slot's place adds to its total.
- Slots empty every turn, and End Turn discards your hand.
- Generic dice can't start a slot, but they power up any slot with an action.
- When an elite or boss falls, the rest of its crew flees.

## Ink
- Insight is now ink. Click the inkwell to open the log, then use the quill to:
  - reroll a die
  - redraw a die
  - see an enemy's exact numbers
  - make an enemy choose a different move
- Unspent ink carries over to the next turn.

## The map
- Every page you draft comes with a sea stop after its island:
  - **Calm water:** heal
  - **Shipwright's buoy:** the workbench
  - **The wreck:** a core
  - **Whetstone reef:** +1 minimum roll on a die
  - **Flotsam:** gold
  - **Squall:** remove a die
- Take a page: one of the others burns away, and the last is yours.
- A harbour before each boss: rest or refit.
- Each chapter is 3 spreads, then the boss.
- Your ship sails the chart from stop to stop.

## A new look
- A logbook style throughout: paper cards, ink, slot tickets and intent tags.
- Dice drawn with real pips. Multitype dice show both colours, special dice have a rarity ring, and a few dice have a look of their own.
- Screen shake (it can be turned off in Settings), hit flashes, death animations, room transitions and new sounds.
- Reward cards flip over to reveal their rarity.

## Easier to learn
- A new guided first fight. Settings > Tutorial next run replays it.
- Keywords, buffs and debuffs are explained in tooltips and shown as icons on dice.
- Enemy intents say what they do and to whom.
- Hover a die in a slot to see how its total adds up.
- The workbench previews what each tool will make.
- Shorter, clearer text everywhere.

## Starting a voyage
- The quartermaster's offer: choose a starting die with a core already fitted.

## Balance
- You start with 60 health.
- Enemy attacks hit about 20% harder, but normal enemies have about 20% less health.
- Sparing an enemy pays a third of its bounty.
- Calm water is the only way to heal between fights.
- Bosses always reward rare dice and rare cores.
- Rot now halves one slot's total.
- Grog of Grit: +3 Might for one turn.
- Dice and keepsakes reworked for rolled dice, including Topographic Map, Repeater Rail and Balancing Compass.
- Compass Core and Lodestone Core replace the Odd and Even cores. They shape a die by your alignment.

## Content
- New dice: Gunpowder, Tidecaller, Ballast.
- New events: A floating crate, The tattooist.
- The bag view can be sorted and filtered.

## Fixes
- A crash when ending a turn while Mocked.
- Many smaller fixes from playtest feedback.
