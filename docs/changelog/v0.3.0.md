# Tides of Fortune v0.3.0

Enemies telegraph more, status effects finally make sense, and the workbench is now a proper bench. Start a new voyage to see it all.

## Combat
- **Buffs and debuffs rebuilt.** Every status shows its amount on its icon, for you and for enemies. Hover one for the total and what it does.
- **Enemies plan ahead.** Their moves sit on a timeline above your slots. Bind and Rot mark slot 1 or 2 for the turn, and debuffs like Mock land after all your slots.
- **Overwhelm drains your ink.** New and reworked dice and keepsakes (Bilge-Purge, Eye patch, War Drum and more).
- **Save and quit mid-fight** without changing how the fight plays out.

## The workbench
- A free bench: lay your dice out anywhere and use the tools wherever they land.
- **Hammer** joins two dice, **Cutters** trim one and give a coin, **Drill** fits a core. Each visit gives 2 actions, one per tool use.
- **The Bin** (bought in shops) throws one die out of your bag per visit.
- Tools are sold in every shop.

## Cores
- Alignment cores are simpler and easier to read. A die's tooltip shows its core and what it does.

## The map
- **Drop Anchor** stops: rest or go below to the workbench. There is one before every boss.
- Drafting offers 2 of the 4 pages.
- Each chapter has its own events, 16 of them new, with bigger choices.
- Item rewards grow as you go: after the Chapter 1 boss you pick from 3.

## Polish
- Tooltips share one stacked style and wrap properly. Descriptions use a new slab font.
- Keywords and buffs have tooltips almost everywhere, including shops and rewards.
- Press Tab in a fight to toggle the ink view.
- The death screen shows your score and time.
