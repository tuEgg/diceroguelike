# Rooms: shop, event, bounty, treasure, workbench

Each room has a manager object created by the room; most of its logic and clicks are in that object's Draw GUI. Exit buttons send you back to `rmMap`.

## Shop: `oShopManager`, `scShop`

- `stock_shop()` builds `shop_dice_options`, `shop_consumable_options`, `shop_keepsake_options`, `shop_tool_options` from `oWorldManager.current_node.shop_stock` (names) or rolls fresh (debug jumps). `roll_shop_stock()` rolls with shop odds and returns names.
- `generate_item_rewards` leaves out a core already in your item bar (`item_core_owned`) unless `_allow_owned_cores` (a bounty contract's own reward core); with none of the wanted rarity left it puts them back.
- Reward generators (also used by combat rewards, bounties, treasure): `generate_dice_rewards`, `generate_item_rewards` (both take `_for_room` to choose odds), `generate_keepsake_rewards` (skips owned and the bounty's keepsake), `generate_tool_rewards`. Each has a 500-attempt safety net.

## Events: `oEventManager`, `scEvents`

- `define_events()` builds `global.master_event_list`: structs `{ name, description, options: ds_list }`, each option `{ title, description, effect(ctx), result }`. Effects set `oEventManager.event_complete` and `event_selected`; `alternate_exit` can redirect the exit (Sirens → shop). Alignment changes go through `gain_alignment(amount, x, y)` (scParticles): motes (oParticleAlignment) coloured by `alignment_colour` fly to `alignment_target()` and move the compass as they land. The Siren's Call upgrades by alignment: `sirens_upgrade_count`, `sirens_upgrade`. An option's `description` may be a function (worked out as it's drawn).
- After an option is chosen, the event text is replaced by that option's `result` line, so every option needs a real sentence there (never a placeholder).
- **Chapter pools (F251, 2026-11-24):** `event_chapter(name)` says which chapter an event belongs to (1 unless listed); `enter_node` picks from this chapter's island or sea events, then earlier chapters', then any. Chapter 2 and 3 events are built from the `ev_*` helpers (scEvents, after `define_events`): `ev_done`, `ev_pay`, `ev_gold`, `ev_hurt`, `ev_heal`, `ev_max_hp`, `ev_luck`, `ev_give_keepsakes/dice/item/tool`, `ev_pick` (the dice picker, up to N), `ev_remove_pick` (never below 6 dice), `ev_upgrade_die`, `ev_min_roll`. The picker's `upgrade` filter refuses coins and d12s.
- `generate_event()` picks the node's remembered `event_name`, or a debug-forced one, or random, and removes it from the list (seen events are saved).
- Hovering a choice explains the keywords, buffs, debuffs and game words in it (`queue_glossary_tooltip`; the game words are `mechanics_glossary`, scHelpers). Many option effects open a dice picker via `oRunManager.dice_selection` and `dice_selection_event`.
- Dice an event hands you are `oDice` in hand: a click sends one to the bag as an `oDiceParticle` (oDice Step, F149), except while the plank waits (`deleting_die == 1`), when they drag.

**Look (UI rework):** events draw as a paper page (`ui_paper`, `ui_paper_text`) with option slips; every room leaves through `ui_primary` "Continue" bottom right (`exit_btn` is its { hover, click }).

## Bounty board: `oBountyController` (persistent), `scBounties`

- Created once per run in `rmMap`; drawn only in `rmBounty`. Offers 2 contracts (intended) and a free potion, drawn as the "On the house" card bottom left (oBountyController Draw GUI: `potion`, a Take it button with `potion_btn_scale`, greyed when `oRunManager.has_space_for_item` is false; a Taken stamp after).
- Contract `{ enemy_name, condition, rewards [item, keepsake, die], elite_encounter, complete }`; conditions in `condition_list` have `trigger(event, data)` that sets `failed`. Taking one sets `oRunManager.active_bounty`; the bounty elite replaces `possible_elites` until fought.
- Rerolling the board (10 gold) clears `oRunManager.active_bounty`, so a contract can only be active while it is on the board. Contract names come from `generate_bounties` (Elite 1 and Elite 3 are both Pirate Captain fights).
- Help (F249): first-time tips `bounty_intro`, `bounty_house`, `bounty_reroll` (rmBounty), `bounty_bar` (rmMap, the top-bar contract), `bounty_fight` (rmCombat). In the contract's elite (`combat_is_bounty_fight`) oCombat Draw GUI draws a banner under the top bar from `bounty_contract_status`: the rule, a live count, amber at the limit, red once broken. The board only appears from chapter 2 once an elite is beaten (map-and-chapters.md, the chapter pool).
- Saved with the run: `serialise_bounty_board` / `deserialise_bounty_board`.

## Treasure: `oTreasureManager`

One keepsake from the node's `treasure_keepsake` (or rolled), revealed by opening a chest. Continue with the chest closed or the keepsake untaken asks first: it reads "Leave it?" (danger frame) for 3 s (`exit_confirm` frames) and a second click leaves (B136).

## Workbench: `oWorkbenchManager` (the free bench, F224)

- **No slots** (Jared, 2026-11-22; mockup `docs/mockups/workbench-freeform.html`). The manager draws everything in Draw GUI and handles input in Step: a plank table (`table`), a pegboard rack on the right (`rack`) with the tools you own, the dice on the table as structs (`wb_dice`: { die, x, y, scale (springy, `sv`), rot, flash, hop, vx, vy, fly }), drawn by `wb_draw_die` with `draw_die` (a die with a core has its distribution drawn under it, `draw_dice_distribution`). No `oDice` instances here, and the old `oDrill` / `oHammer` / `oScissors` objects aren't created any more (their sprites are drawn; the objects only identify tools in `oRunManager.tools`).
- **Dice:** the bag icon (any time) or the note on the bench opens the picker for as many dice as you like (`workbench_open_picker`, up-to mode, the whole bag); `workbench_take_die` deals each onto a free spot as a fight deals (`fly` kind "deal": 0.1 s apart, the roll sound, tumbling, slowing to land; no face). Drag dice anywhere; they nudge apart so they never stack. Drop one on the bag to put it back. Continue sends every die on the table back (oDiceParticle adds each as it lands) and returns a loaded core to the top bar.
- **Tools** (`wb_tools`: kind, sprite, hx/hy = the working point: the drill's tip, the hammer's head, where the blades cross; each is held by its grip, `wb_tool_vec` (the drill just above its chuck, the hammer low on its handle, the cutters in the middle), and turns about it: `wb_tool_grip`, `wb_tool_place`, `wb_tool_origin` / `wb_tool_box` at `wb_tool_k` scale; dice within 80 px of the working point count). Grab one, carry it, let go: `wb_tool_target` says what it would do there (shown by the tool, with brass or red rings on the dice), and a valid drop starts `wb_job` (an animation whose craft happens on its impact frame). Right click or the rack puts a tool back. Misses cost nothing. Each visit gives 2 actions (`actions_left`, two brass bars across the rack's width, F246): a craft spends `wb_cost(kind)` (Hammer and Bin 2, Drill and Cutters 1; `workbench_tool_used(_obj, _cost)`); a tool you can't afford greys out and every tooltip says "Uses N actions." (`wb_cost_text`). **The Bin** (kind "bin" in `wb_tools`, but not a tool you carry: a fixed `bin_area` under the rack, icon from `draw_bin_icon`): drop a die on it (`wb_in_bin`) and the "bin" job removes it from the bag (2 actions, never below 6 dice; it counts for `workbench_useful`).
- Holding a tool shows its how-to under the cursor (oWorkbenchManager Draw GUI, by `wb_held.ref.kind`), besides `wb_target`'s text for what it would do where it is. A die's distribution here is the paper-chip style of the bag cards (`draw_dice_distribution(..., _paper, 999, _plate)`).
- **Drill:** drag a core from the top bar onto the drill (`workbench_take_core`, called by oRunManager on release; `wb_socket` is the chuck), then drop the drill on a die (not a coin): it gets the core's `distribution`. **Hammer:** let go over the gap between two dice at most 240 px apart, their gap within 110 px (`wb_pair_at`): same action type and 12 sides or fewer, they become one die (sizes added, less 2: d4 + d4 = d6, F236; `die_size_changed`) under the head; the die with a core carries on (one of the two cores at random if both have one); otherwise they jump apart. The swing (also for a miss) tips the head back to the right, then thwacks it down to the left, anticlockwise to 65 degrees; the hit is on the downswing, frame 14, with the head on its target; its mark on the table fades over 3 s (`wb_scuffs`). **Cutters:** drop on a die bigger than a coin: it loses 2 sides (`die_size_changed`; cut to a coin, its core is lost) and a fresh coin of its type hops out beside it.
- Tips: `workbench_bag` (the note), `workbench` (the rack), `workbench_drill` (`workbench_drill_waits`: an empty drill and a core in your items).
- **At sea (F02):** finishing a page (oWorldManager Step, when `nodes_til_page_cleared` hits 0) sets `oRunManager.can_visit_workbench`; whether a tool has work (`workbench_useful()`: the Hammer or Cutters, or the Drill with a core in your items) is checked when the buoy or strait shows; the map floats a buoy on the way to the next island (`sea_buoy_draw`, see map-and-chapters.md), or at a spread's end the buoy is one of the strait's stops; sailing past clears it.
