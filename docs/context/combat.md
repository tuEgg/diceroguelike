# Combat

Room `rmCombat`, object `oCombat` (not persistent). Created with the enemies `oWorldManager.room_enemies` holds (filled by `get_combat_enemies` in scMap, or by the debug menu via `debug_apply_pending_fight`).

## Turn loop: `oCombat/Step_0.gml`

`state` (enum `CombatState`): `START_TURN` → `PLAYER_INPUT` → `RESOLVE_ROUND` → `END_OF_ROUND` → back to `START_TURN`; `PLAYER_DEAD` on death.

**Rolled dice prototype (branch `feature/rolled-dice`, rules in `docs/design-rolled-dice.md`).** Dice roll when dealt (`die_roll_faces` in scDice, called by `deal_single_die`; the result is `faces` on the die struct, cleared by `die_clear_faces` when it's discarded). `process_action` resolves those faces instead of rolling (a forced roll still wins; a keepsake's roll-twice rolls once more and keeps the higher). Per turn on oCombat: `played_uids` (when-played effects fire once per die per turn), rerolls and redraws cost insight as you do them (below). `combat_turn_tokens_draw` (oCombat Draw GUI, above End Turn) shows plays, rerolls and redraws as one row of tokens, with a tooltip per group. Played dice carry `played_turn` and `play_locked`; Draw GUI sets `take_back_request` when one is clicked and Step runs `combat_take_back`. The guided first fight is back on (F153), rewritten for rolled dice and ink (below). In fights dice are drawn in code by `draw_die` (scDice; shape per size, pips): oDice Draw GUI for the hand, oCombat Draw GUI for slots and the new-slot tile. A dealt die tumbles (oDice `roll_anim`, `tumbling`, `flick_face`, `land_pop`; `no_tumble` on dice taken back). No die-size rule for joining a slot any more.

- **START_TURN**: picks each enemy's move with `enemy_pick_move_index` (scEnemies; `move_order`: `true_random`, `ordered`, `weighted` via `choose_weighting_list`, `pseudo_random` = no 4 in a row). `use_trigger` on a move overrides: `FIRST` (turn 1), `HEALTH 50` (once, flagged `trigger_used` on the per-fight move copy), `PRIORITY`. Fires `on_turn_start`, sets intel (`debug_turn_intel()` + buffs), works out `intel_level`, deals dice (`dice_to_deal`).
- **PLAYER_INPUT**: waits for `actions_submitted` (Enter or the End Turn button in Draw GUI).
- **RESOLVE_ROUND**: discards dice still in hand, then each slot's dice roll one by one through `process_action`. Before slot N+1 starts, every enemy whose move is set after slot N acts (`combat_enemy_due`, `combat_enemy_act`); after your last slot the rest act one at a time. See **The timeline** below.
- **END_OF_ROUND**: a Sticky die stays one extra turn (`sticky_held`; it acts again, then leaves); turns every other die temporary and ejects it (`eject_dice_in_slot`), `slot_tick_bonuses`, `slot_recompute_types` (back to the types the slot was built with), discards dice left on the new-slot tile (`combat_discard_slot_building`), removes built slots (`combat_queue_reset_built`: all but the first, unless a Sticky die holds one; place bonuses set again), resets enemy block (unless `keep_block_between_turns`), fades out dead enemies (and ones that fled), then `win_fight()` or `on_round_end` and the next turn.

`oCombat/Draw_64.gml` (~1500 lines) draws everything and handles most clicks: slots, dice in slots (with slot-bonus badges), enemies, intents, buffs, rewards screen.
- Intel: `player_intel` can go negative (Overwhelm). The intel ring above the player is clamped between empty and full; the number shows whenever intel isn't zero, red when negative.

## Rolling and damage: `process_action` (scCombatScripts)

`process_action(target, dice_amount, dice_value, bonus, source, source_index, type, slot_number, slot_die, dice_list_index)`

- Player rolls go through `get_dice_output` (fires `on_roll_die`: keepsakes and buffs adjust min/max/bonus), a weighted distribution (cores), `after_roll_die`, then add the slot's every-die bonus and, for the slot's first die, `first_die_bonus`.
- `get_dice_output` is also used for previews (hover, bag, slot ranges): previews pass `read_only` true so keepsakes and dice don't change state or pop, and a die not in a slot is previewed as its own action type so type bonuses show before it is played.
- Enemy rolls: plain range, then the roller's own buffs on `on_roll_die` (ctx `owner` and `target` are both the roller).
- Types: `ATK` (block absorbs post-modifier damage; `before_enemy_take_damage`, `on_enemy_take_damage`, death handling), `BLK`, `HEAL`, `DEBUFF`/`BUFF` (apply_buff to target, `remove_next_turn` true), `INTEL`, `SUMMON`. `dice_amount` 0 means flat damage of `dice_value`.

## Slots (the action queue)

`action_queue` is a ds_list of slot structs made by `combat_new_slot(types)`: `dice_list`, `current_action_type`, `possible_type` (space-separated types string), `defined_type` (always "None" now: every slot is untyped again after End Turn), `position_bonus` (`slot_set_position_bonus`: +index once to its total, a "first"-scope slot bonus named "Slot N"), `bonus_amount`, `first_die_bonus`, `bonuses`.

- **Look (UI rework):** the fight is raised (`global.enemy_y` 638, the queue at `gui_h / 2 - 92`, so health bars clear the tray's edge); the queue keeps 150 px left of the nearest enemy and right of the player (oCombat Draw GUI), and stores `aq_left` / `aq_mid_y`; the inkwell sits just before the queue (`combat_inkwell_pos()`, read by the HUD, the ink view and the tips). A reshuffle of the discard pile plays `combat_shuffle_effect` (look-only `oDiceParticle`s, `visual_only`). slots are `ui_ticket`s (oCombat Draw GUI), the new-slot tile a `ui_dashed_box`, intents sit on `ui_tag`s, HP is `ui_hp_bar`. End Turn is `ui_primary_button` bottom right (`end_turn_draw` is its rect {x1, y1, x2, y2}; the plays row sits above it); the discard pile is bottom left beside the draw bag (`combat_discard_spot`), and there is no drop-to-discard zone. Placing: `slot_placement_check(die_struct, slot, slot_i)` is the one rule. It returns `{ ok, action, reason }`: action "claim" (empty slot takes the die's type), "replace" (an Exclusive die clears a slot of another type) or "add"; `reason` is player-facing text for why not. `apply_dice_to_slot` acts on it. The slot highlight asks the same rule through `can_place_dice_in_slot`, so what lights up is exactly what accepts the die. The hover hint while dragging uses it too: a "Can't place here" tooltip with the reason, or what placing will do; a rejected drop shows the reason with `throw_error`. Taking back a die that built its slot (`built_slot`) takes the slot apart: `combat_unbuild_slot` (the die to hand, the other builders back onto the new-slot tile, dice played into it since to hand, Shipwright's Draft's play taken back via `draft_bonus`), allowed by `combat_built_die_can_return` only for the newest slot built this turn whose `undo_ok` holds (no builder with a "when sacrificed" effect, or a "when played" one with Lead Line). `combat_die_to_hand` returns any die to the hand. Taking a die back: `combat_take_back_blocked(slot_i, index)` is the order rule (a slot can't lose its action while a later slot has dice; a built slot with its own `defined_type`, or one keeping another coloured die, never blocks), and the die's tooltip shows its reason instead of "Click to take it back". Write new rules into `slot_placement_check` with a reason the player can read. Shortcut: hover a die in hand and press 1-9 (or the numpad) to call `apply_dice_to_slot` on that slot (oDice Step), the same as dropping it there.
- Keyword rules in combat (Exclusive, Sticky, Loose ejection, Coin not counting as a played die) read the die's `keywords` via `die_has_keyword`, never its description.
- A new slot can only be built once every slot (but a chained one) has an action (`combat_all_slots_have_action`, checked in `sacrifice_die` and `combat_try_build_slot`; the tile says "Fill slots first").
- Every turn starts with one slot (the queue resets, F193). Slots fill left to right: `slot_placement_check` refuses a slot while an earlier one (not `locked_slot`) lacks `slot_has_action`, and `combat_take_back` won't empty a slot while a later one has dice.
- New slots: `sacrifice_die` puts a die on the new-slot tile (`global.sacrifice_list`, uses a play); `combat_try_build_slot` builds the slot once `new_slot_min_dice()` dice reach `new_slot_threshold()` (`new_slot_total()` adds their rolled numbers). The slot gets their types for this turn only (`slot_recompute_types`); "when sacrificed" effects fire then, with a stand-in `{ struct, x, y }` as `data.die`. The dice move into the new slot (`play_locked`, `built_slot`) and act this turn.
- Elites and bosses: when one dies (`enemy_is_leader`), `combat_enemies_flee` marks the rest `dead`, `fled` and `looted` (no gold); END_OF_ROUND fades fled enemies out before the kill/spare handling.
- **The timeline (F193, Jared 2026-11-22).** Each enemy move happens after one of your slots: `enemy.intent.after_slot`, set in START_TURN by `enemy_move_point(move)` (scEnemies): a move's `timing` is "first" (1), "last" (`ENEMY_POINT_LAST` 99) or a slot number (Peg-leg's Escaping: 2); otherwise rolled 1 to `ENEMY_POINT_ROLL_MAX` (4). A point past your built slots acts at the end. Each enemy gets a letter and colour for the fight (`enemy.mark` from `oCombat.mark_next`; `combat_enemy_colour`, `combat_enemy_badge`), shown on its intent tag and its marker. `combat_timeline_draw` (oCombat Draw GUI, and over the ink view) draws the track over the queue from `aq_x0`, `aq_y0`, `aq_step`: numbered notches, a marker per enemy at the gap after its slot (`combat_point_x`), stacked when they share one; dimmed once acted, greyed if dead or Stunned. Ink: Earlier / Later under each intent for `INK_COST_MOVE` (4) moves `after_slot` by one (cap 7; Earlier from "last" counts from your built slots). A Rewrite keeps the point. `enemy_point_text` words it for tooltips. A death or flee only counts an enemy off `enemy_turns_remaining` if it hasn't acted. Bind and Rot pick their slot at turn start, so with one slot they hit slot 1.
- **Slot bonuses**: list of `{ source, amount, turns, scope }`. `scope` "each" (every die, the default) or "first" (first die only). Use `slot_add_bonus` / `slot_set_bonus` / `slot_remove_bonus`; `slot_tick_bonuses` at end of round; `slot_total_bonus(slot, dice)` for display; `slot_bonus_breakdown` for the hover box. `source` is shown to the player, so use the keepsake/item name.

## Buffs and debuffs: scDebuffs

- Templates are instance variables made by `define_buffs_and_debuffs()` on `oRunManager` (`oRunManager.buff_might` etc.).
- `apply_buff(target, template, duration, amount, remove_next_turn, source, permanent)` clones the template into `{ template, remaining, permanent, amount, remove_next_turn, source_info }` in `global.player_debuffs` or `enemy.debuffs`. Merges only with the same id **and** same permanence (and, if stackable, same `remove_next_turn`).
- `trigger_debuff_list(list, event, ctx)` fires effects, then counts down once per buff on its `removal_event`.
- A new slot needs `new_slot_threshold()` pips: 2 x (slot number - 1) plus `oCombat.slot_cost_modifier`, in pips (Cursed Heart -2), never below 1.
- Generic (neutral) dice can't start a slot (B36). On the new-slot tile they can join once a coloured die is there (`die_cant_build_slot_reason`, `new_slot_has_coloured`; Jared 2026-11-21), and a slot is only built with a coloured die on it. The tile shows what it needs: "N+ pips" inside, and above it a faded d6 outline for each die still to come (`new_slot_min_dice`), which dropped dice fill.
- Might/Balance check `owner == target`.

## Intents and intel

- Every enemy's intent (what it will do this turn) is shown. `enemy_set_intent(enemy, move)` sets it and **rolls its dice then** (`intent.rolls`); the enemy's turn uses those rolls (`process_action`), so the exact number shown in advance is what happens. Never set `intent.move` directly. Moves are picked by `enemy_pick_move_index` (scEnemies), then `use_trigger` moves override.
- Insight (`player_intel`) is spent during your turn (`combat_buy` / `combat_insight_spend`, costs are the `INSIGHT_COST_` macros in scEnemies): exact enemy intents 2 (click the eye; `insight_revealed` for the turn, which sets `intel_level` to INTEL_LEGIBLE, otherwise INTEL_FADED), another reroll or redraw 3 (`rerolls_bought`, `redraws_bought`), another play 4 (`plays_bought`, added to `dice_allowed_per_turn` in Step), a Quill rewrite 6. You can't spend while Blotted (below 0). **The insight phase** (Jared, 2026-11-19: insight is ink, and you're rewriting the captain's log). The log never opens by itself: once the hand is dealt (oCombat Step, PLAYER_INPUT, `insight_phase_done`), if `combat_insight_phase_worth()` the HUD inkwell pulses for 2.5 s (`ink_pulse`). Clicking the inkwell toggles it: `combat_insight_view_set` (UI_LAYER.POPUP). `combat_insight_view_draw` (first in oRunManager Draw GUI End) draws the screen as ink on parchment (F118: oRunManager Draw GUI Begin, Draw_74, draws the whole frame into `oRunManager.ink_scene` while the log is open, `ink_capturing`; Draw GUI End resets the target first thing; the view draws `ink_scene` through the `shInk` shader, with its overlay `insight_surf` as `u_mask`; without the shader, or on the frame the log opens, it darkens instead) except holes over each `intent_rect` and the action queue's pieces, each cut to its own shape (`combat_queue_holes_draw`: every slot to its own sprite, dot included, and its dice, the new-slot tile, the End Turn sprite itself, the token row), which stay in full colour; End Turn reads "Confirm" in its own place under the slots (oCombat Draw GUI) and its click is taken by the view (`end_turn_draw`, not over a slot); on top: the inkwell (`draw_inkwell`), each intent split at `intent_rect.split` (move: reroll for 6, `enemy_rewrite_intent`; numbers: `combat_reveal_enemy` for `combat_reveal_cost()`, 2 alone or 1 each), and each die in hand drawn again on top with a yellow edge (a die with an ink effect, like Deepdiver, also gets a white halo and an ink drop, and its tooltip adds its description) (left-click reroll 3: `combat_reroll_die`; right-click redraw 3: `combat_redraw_die`; mouse hints above it when hovered). Every ink action has a full tooltip with its cost tag (`intent_verbs` words the enemy's move). The cursor is a quill (`draw_quill`, via `cursor_draw`) only while `quill_over` (the view sets it over an intent it can change or a die); otherwise the normal cursor. "Confirm", the inkwell or Enter closes it; right-click only redraws a die. Nothing insight-related happens in the main turn: no right-click reroll, plays can't be bought, and dice can't be discarded (dropping one on the discard pile says to redraw it with ink; only `combat_redraw_die` discards). Balance earned for next turn (Balanced die) waits in `balance_next_turn` and is applied as the next turn starts. Ink (`player_intel`) carries over between turns, capped at INSIGHT_MAX (12) at the start of a turn; insight gained during a turn arrives next turn as `buff_intel` (insight dice, Eye patch). The view closes when your turn ends; Enter doesn't end the turn while it's open. `insight_panel_draw` (the old price list) is no longer opened. Tooltips for anything that costs pass `_cost` / `_cost_kind` to `queue_tooltip`, drawn as a "-N" tag on the corner (`tooltip_cost_badge`). `intel_max()` is INSIGHT_MAX (12), the gauge's top.
- The number beside an intent comes from `intent_number_text(enemy, intel_level)`: the range when Faded, `intent_exact_value` (rolls + bonus + the enemy's Might/Balance) from Legible, "?" when Blotted (`intent_range_text` for the range). Hovering an enemy shows `intent_hover_text` ("Attacks you for 4-9"), not the dice. You can always change target (click an enemy); with 2+ enemies standing the target has a red "Target" marker under its health bar and others say "Click to target".
- Insight dice roll when their slot resolves, like any die, and queue `buff_intel` ("Queued insight"), which adds to `player_intel` at the start of next turn. Insight over two turns (Radar die) is `buff_intel_lasting` ("Lasting insight", duration 2), kept apart so it never merges with the one-turn queue. Rot (`debuff_rot`) picks `bound_slot` each turn; `process_action` halves that slot's total die by die (`rot_spare` carries the odd point) and `get_slot_stats` shows it halved. Crow's Nest, the Lookout die and the sacrifice die use it too. (Rolling on play, and counting this turn only, were both tried and reverted.)
- Quill (now "reroll this intent", in insight view): 6 insight, the enemy picks a different, untriggered move, rolled fresh (`enemy_rewrite_intent`). Halve and the old under-intent menu (`quill_menu_enemy`) are switched off (`_can_rewrite`, `_rewritable` false in oCombat Draw_64); `enemy_halve_intent` is still there, unused. Forced moves and Stunned can't be rerolled (`enemy_can_rewrite_intent`).
- On screen intel is called **Ink** (2026-11-22; it was Insight): all player-facing text says ink, and `action_type_label` (scDice) turns INTEL into INK. Its colour is an inky black (`global.color_intel`); `global.text_intel` is the pale ink for words and numbers on dark surfaces, `text_intel_on_paper` near black; the old yellow is `global.color_gold`, for plain highlights. The ink view (`combat_insight_view_draw`) labels every action: `ink_button` pills over each die (Reroll, Redraw) and under each intent (Reveal, Rewrite), each with its cost; `draw_ink_pips` shows ink under the inkwell (in and out of the view), flickering what a hovered button would spend (`oCombat.ink_preview`); `float_text` shows what changed. End Turn reads "Done" in the view. The code, slot types and `player_intel` keep the old name. The eye is the sIntelEye sprite: frame 0 Blotted, 1 ranges, 2 exact (`index` in `global.player_intel_data` / `global.intel_blotted`); it breathes while seeing exactly is affordable.
- Overwhelm (`debuff_overwhelm`, Driftnet Fish and the Parrot's mimic) is applied on the enemy's turn with `remove_next_turn`, so it stays on you through your next turn (removed `on_round_end`). At that turn's start it lowers insight and sets `target_locked` (reset in START_TURN); clicking another enemy is refused while the locked target stands.

## Tutorial (guided first fight)

Combat speed (F13): `action_delay` and `dice_delay` are `*_delay_base` times `combat_delay_scale()` (scSettings; Settings > Gameplay > Combat speed), recomputed each Step. oCombat Step does nothing while the pause menu is open.

scCombatScripts, TUTORIAL section. While `progress.json` has `tutorial_done` false, `get_combat_enemies` makes the first combat node a lone Pirate Ruffian and `deal_single_die` deals a set first hand from the bag (`tutorial_next_hand_type` returns `{ type, face }`; the face is forced through `forced_roll` so each step works: Attack 3 and 2, generic 2, Block 4 to build slot 2, insight 2, Block 1). Steps (F153): read the intent, rolled dice, claim, stack, generic, take back (`global.tut.took_back`, set in `combat_take_back`), build slot 2, its place bonus, end turn; turn 2: open the inkwell (ink topped up to 6 by the step's `enter`), reroll or redraw (`global.tut.inked`), the enemy side of ink, then free. A step may have `enter()` (run once as it comes up) and `covers` (a one-time tip it teaches, marked seen by `tutorial_finish` once passed). While the ink view is open the note is drawn over it from `tips_draw` (`tutorial_draw(true)`). `tutorial_begin` (oCombat Create) holds the steps in `global.tut`; `tutorial_step` (Step) moves on when a step's `done()` is true; `tutorial_draw` (end of Draw GUI) draws the note, arrow and highlight and handles Next and Skip. `tutorial_allows(action, die, slot)` gates dropping dice (oDice Step) and ending the turn; refused moves explain why. Notes point at rects from `tutorial_target_rect` (`slot_positions`, `tut_last_tile`, `tut_end_turn` stored by Draw GUI). Skip, winning, losing or leaving the fight calls `tutorial_finish`. The fight runs over two turns (`hand` then `hand2`); the right-click step listens for `global.tut.switched`, set where oCombat Draw GUI switches a slot's action. Notes are drawn by `tutorial_draw_note(text, rect, button)`, shared with the map: `tips_draw` (oRunManager Draw GUI End, every room) shows the map notes (`tutorial_map_notes`: offering, drafting, Embark, sailing; only while the tutorial is wanted), then the one-time tips in `tips_list` (`room`, optional `when`/`done`, `target` via `tips_rect`, a "Got it" button). Add a tip there. Skip (`tutorial_skip`) marks all tips seen. The insight hover panel is drawn by `insight_panel_draw` in oRunManager Draw GUI End, after the notes (oCombat Draw GUI sets `insight_panel_open`), so it covers them.

## Layout and feedback

- Enemy positions: `enemy_layout_update()` (oCombat Step) centres the enemies by how many are in the list now, spreads them (first in the list on the right), nudges them right as the queue grows (kept on screen), staggers y, sets sizes from the count, and lerps `pos_x`/`pos_y` there. New enemies snap on their first step (`layout_placed`).
- Roll feedback: `roll_popup(slot, dice_index, text, colour)` floats a word over a slot ("Warrior!"); a roll-twice shows both faces (`weighted_to_face`). `after_roll_die` ctx also has `first_bonus` (the slot's first-die bonus when this is that die), so effects can judge the total (Bilge).
- Potions used on a slot (Rigger's Tonic, Bilge-Purge): oRunManager calls `combat_item_drop(item)` on release (slot under the mouse via `combat_slot_under_mouse`); slots pulse while one is carried (`combat_carrying_slot_item`).
- Dealing: `generate_valid_targets` keeps each new die's spot clear of the dice already in hand.

## Enemies: scEnemies

Intent words (scEnemies): `intent_hover_text` says what a move does; buffs and debuffs go through `intent_status_text(move)` ("Puts Mock on you for 1 turn. Mock: ..."; a buff on itself, or its allies with target "other"), also used by the enemy hover in oCombat Draw GUI. A SUMMON has no number (`intent_has_numbers` false) and draws `ui_summon_icon` (scUI). While you're Mocked, `die_is_mocked(die)` (scDebuffs) marks dice that can attack with `draw_mock_mark` (oDice Draw GUI and the ink view), and the reroll hover warns.

`define_enemies()` builds `global.enemy_list` via `enemy_create(...)` (moves are structs: `dice_amount, dice_value, action_type, bonus_amount, move_name`, optional `weight, use_trigger, debuff, amount, duration, target, summon`). `add_enemy_to_fight` calls `enemy_copy_for_fight` then `enemy_apply_chapter_scaling` (scMap). Per-fight lists are freed in `oCombat/CleanUp_0` via `fight_enemy_records`.

## Effect events

`combat_trigger_effects(event, ctx)` → dice in slots (or just `ctx._die_struct`), keepsakes (`runmanager_trigger_keepsakes`), player buffs, each enemy's buffs, bounty conditions. Event names are listed in `docs/context/dice-keepsakes-items.md`.
